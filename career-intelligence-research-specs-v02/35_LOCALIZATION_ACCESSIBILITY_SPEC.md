# Localization and accessibility

Make recommendations usable across languages, geographies, devices and accessibility needs.

## Localization

Language, labor-market taxonomy, currency, salary conventions, legal regime, tool availability, local job sources and education providers.

## Accessibility

Keyboard navigation; semantic HTML; screen-reader support; contrast; captions/transcripts; reduced motion; clear error recovery; plain language.

## Data

Never assume a tool or job source is globally available. Availability is a versioned property.
