# V02 Roadmap & Research Backlog

## Product layers
1. Career Intelligence — who am I, where can I move, what opportunities fit?
2. Work Intelligence — what tasks and workflows make up my current work?
3. AI Transformation Intelligence — what changes at task/workflow level?
4. AI Enablement — what do I need to learn and what tools fit?
5. Implementation — how do I configure or delegate setup?
6. Outcome Intelligence — did it work, at what cost and risk?
7. Opportunity Creation — what new roles/services/businesses become possible?

## MVP sequencing
### Phase 1: Foundation
Profession taxonomy, profile, task map, AI capability taxonomy, evidence/provenance, safety model.

### Phase 2: First value
Beginner onboarding, one-task diagnosis, three validated workflows for one pilot profession, guided verification.

### Phase 3: Enablement
Tool catalog, learning paths, documentation, DIY/guided/done-for-you package cards.

### Phase 4: Implementation
Connector support, workflow setup, pilot telemetry, cost/ROI and human support.

### Phase 5: Scale
More professions, regional localization, provider marketplace, agent workflows, aggregated benchmarks.

## Critical unknowns
- Which initial profession and country provide enough reliable data?
- Which first workflow has high frequency, low risk and measurable value?
- Is the business model SaaS, service, marketplace or hybrid?
- Which tool vendors permit reliable integrations and local access?
- What evidence is available for task time, error rates and willingness to pay?
- How will professional liability be handled in regulated/safety-critical work?

## Research protocol
Prioritize interviews and workflow observation over broad surveys. Validate actual tasks, current tools, failure points, budget, willingness to pay and adoption barriers.

## Kill criteria
Stop or redesign a workflow if measured value is negative, review burden is high, user adoption is low, risks cannot be controlled or a simpler non-AI process is better.
