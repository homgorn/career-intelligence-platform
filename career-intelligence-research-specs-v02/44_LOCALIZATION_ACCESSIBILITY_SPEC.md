# Localization & Accessibility Specification

## Purpose
Ensure the product works for people outside major English-speaking technology markets and for users with limited digital access.

## Localization dimensions
Language of interface, AI model language quality, speech recognition, OCR, documentation, support, billing currency, payment methods, local law, data residency, local software ecosystem and tool availability.

## Geography
Do not infer a tool is available because its website loads. Verify signup, payment, features and support by country. Provide "not verified" state.

## Accessibility
Mobile-first, low bandwidth, voice input, captions/transcripts, keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, readable contrast, plain language and downloadable instructions.

## Language quality
Test profession-specific vocabulary, local units, abbreviations, names of standards and mixed-language speech. Include a human correction path for transcription errors.

## Acceptance criteria
Every recommendation records country and language verification; every key workflow has an accessible alternative.
