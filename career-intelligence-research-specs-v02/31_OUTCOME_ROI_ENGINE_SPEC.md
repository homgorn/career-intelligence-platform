# Outcome and ROI engine

Calculate expected and observed value without pretending uncertain benefits are exact.

## Expected value

Use ranges and confidence. Inputs may include hourly cost, task frequency, baseline cycle time, quality/error cost, implementation cost, recurring tool cost and maintenance.

## Observed value

Compare baseline period with post-implementation period. Track confounders and user-reported versus instrumented measures separately.

## Decision bands

High value/low risk → implement; high value/high risk → human review/pilot; low value/low risk → optional; low value/high risk → do not implement.
