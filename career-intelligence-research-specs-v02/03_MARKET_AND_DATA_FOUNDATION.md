# Market & Data Foundation

## O*NET
O*NET 31.0 covers occupations, alternate titles, tasks, work activities, work context, skills, knowledge, abilities, education, experience/training, licensing and outlook.

2026 updates expanded work-context research from 57 to 81 measurable dimensions, including remote work, digital collaboration, travel, screen exposure, workload and work design.

## ESCO
3,039 occupations and 13,939 skills, 28 languages, occupation↔skill relationships.

## Lightcast
34,000+ skills; specialized/common/software skills and certifications. 2026 DDN framework adds Necessary / Defining / Distinguishing skills.

## Market snapshot
Every market metric needs:
source + date + geography + methodology + confidence.

Separate:
Fact / Forecast / Inference.

## Key rule
Occupation profile and market snapshot are different entities.
