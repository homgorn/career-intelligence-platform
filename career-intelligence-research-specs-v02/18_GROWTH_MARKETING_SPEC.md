# Marketing and growth system

Acquisition architecture aligned to measurable customer outcomes.

## Positioning

Sell the job-to-be-done: understand what to do next, what AI can change, and how to implement it. Avoid positioning as a generic AI chatbot or generic career quiz.

## Channels

SEO; programmatic landing pages; expert content; YouTube; social; communities; partnerships; outbound B2B; paid search/social; PR; referrals.

## Funnel

Impression → visit → value proof → profile/diagnostic → first result → first implementation → paid/partner conversion → outcome → referral.

## Experiment discipline

Every channel has a target CAC, activation rate, payback and quality threshold. Kill channels that create cheap but low-value traffic.
