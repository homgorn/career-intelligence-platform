# Human review and escalation

Define where automation must stop and a qualified human takes over.

## Triggers

High-impact decision; legal uncertainty; sensitive data; irreversible action; low confidence; conflicting evidence; user dispute; security anomaly.

## Escalation payload

Context, evidence, attempted action, reason, recommended next step, required expertise, deadline.

## Resolution

Human decision is recorded as an auditable event and may update the knowledge base after review.
