# Master research sources

Current external evidence used to shape this architecture. Vendor documentation is used for capability facts; independent/standards sources are used for risk and market framing.

## AI and labor

Anthropic Economic Index: AI use shows both augmentation and automation patterns across tasks; this supports task-level analysis rather than simplistic occupation replacement claims.

## Market/enterprise

McKinsey State of AI 2026 reports increased scaling of agentic AI, especially in large enterprises, while organizations continue to seek ROI. This supports outcome/ROI measurement as a first-class product layer.

## Taxonomy

ESCO provides occupation/skill concepts and APIs for job matching, career guidance and training use cases; latest listed version is 1.2.1 with update in 2025. O*NET remains a key U.S. occupational reference.

## Risk

NIST AI RMF and Generative AI Profile provide a lifecycle risk-management framework. OWASP Top 10:2025 and OWASP LLM guidance provide security threat categories.

## Agent/interop

OpenAI Agents SDK documentation describes tools, handoffs, guardrails, human review and observability. MCP's 2026-07-28 specification adds a stateless protocol core and authorization hardening.

## Search

Google Search Central emphasizes people-first, helpful, reliable content and says the same foundational SEO practices apply to AI search features; it explicitly warns against speculative AI-search hacks.
