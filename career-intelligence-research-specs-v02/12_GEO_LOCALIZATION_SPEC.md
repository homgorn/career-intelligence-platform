# Geography & Localization Specification

## Hierarchy
global → country → region → city → commute zone.

## Geo affects
demand, salary, competition, license, language, training, tax, work authorization, cost of living, remote viability.

## Modes
local / relocation / migration / remote-global.

## Central Asia
Initial modules may cover Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan and remote/global markets, plus migration destinations where legal data is available.

## Language
native / conversational / professional / academic / required.

## Regulatory
country, jurisdiction, license, foreign credential recognition, renewal, scope.

## Market
Every number stores snapshot date and source.
