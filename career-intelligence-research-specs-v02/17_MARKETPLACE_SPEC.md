# Implementation and expertise marketplace

Marketplace connecting users with verified workflows, experts and implementation providers.

## Listings

WorkRecipe; template; course; coach; implementer; agency; integration; managed service.

## Trust

Verification status, evidence, reviews, completion rate, refund rules, SLA, expertise claims, conflict disclosure.

## Transaction

Discovery → eligibility → quote → consent → payment → delivery → verification → outcome → review.

## Avoid

Do not create a directory with no quality signal. Marketplace ranking must incorporate verified outcomes and policy constraints.
