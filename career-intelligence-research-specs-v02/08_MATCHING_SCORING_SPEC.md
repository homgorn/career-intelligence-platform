# Career Matching & Scoring Specification

## Goal
«Насколько эта career option подходит этому человеку при этих ограничениях?»

Not merely resume/JD similarity.

## Dimensions
1 hard eligibility
2 skills
3 task fit
4 experience
5 interests
6 work context
7 motivation/values
8 compensation
9 geography
10 language
11 credentials/licensing
12 training burden
13 transition cost
14 market opportunity
15 future resilience
16 uncertainty.

## Hard constraints
Missing mandatory license, impossible work authorization/location/schedule may produce «not eligible now».

Distinguish:
- not eligible now;
- poor fit;
- good fit with investment.

## Intent-dependent weights
«Нужен доход быстро» and «хочу meaningful career» must not use the same weights.

## Counterfactuals
What if:
- learn skill X;
- move to city Y;
- accept lower salary;
- accept night shifts?

## Explainability
Every recommendation shows positives, blockers, missing evidence, assumptions, confidence, next action.

## Numeric scores
Do not call 87% a probability without calibration. Initially prefer qualitative levels or a clearly non-probabilistic fit index.
