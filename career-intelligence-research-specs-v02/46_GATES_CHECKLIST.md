# Universal gates checklist

Cross-cutting release and feature gates.

## Customer-value gate

What measurable customer problem is solved? What is the baseline? How will improvement be verified?

## Evidence gate

Which claims are verified? What is the source and freshness?

## Safety gate

What can go wrong? Can the system cause irreversible harm? Is human review needed?

## Security gate

What data/tools are exposed? Can tenant isolation or secrets be compromised?

## Legal gate

Which jurisdictions and use cases apply? Has counsel review been triggered where needed?

## Operational gate

Who owns maintenance, support and rollback?

## Economics gate

Expected gross margin and support burden acceptable?

## Evaluation gate

Does the change pass deterministic and AI evaluation suites?
