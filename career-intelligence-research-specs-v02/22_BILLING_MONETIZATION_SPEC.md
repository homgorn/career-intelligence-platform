# Monetization and billing

Pricing must map to delivered value and operational cost.

## Possible models

Free diagnostic; paid individual plan; workflow packs; implementation fee; managed service; B2B seats; enterprise contract; marketplace commission; partner revenue share.

## Billing controls

Entitlements are policy objects. Billing provider is an adapter. Never let payment status alone determine data deletion or legal retention.

## Pricing experiments

Test willingness to pay by outcome and package, not only token/message limits.
