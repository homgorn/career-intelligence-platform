# Research backlog and uncertainty register

Questions that can materially change architecture or GTM must be tracked as research items, not silently assumed.

## Required fields

question; hypothesis; decision affected; evidence needed; source quality; owner; due date; decision threshold; status.

## High-priority unknowns

Best initial ICP; willingness to pay; highest-value profession/workflow; outcome measurement feasibility; local market taxonomy coverage; enterprise procurement friction; tool availability by target geography.

## Rule

Do not build expensive infrastructure to answer an unresolved market question that can be tested manually.
