# AI-Enabled Roles & New Business Opportunities Specification

## Purpose
Identify how AI creates new services, specializations and business models within or adjacent to an existing profession.

## Opportunity types
- existing professional + AI copilot;
- specialist who configures AI for a profession;
- AI-assisted service provider with higher throughput;
- new quality assurance / verification role;
- data and knowledge curator;
- workflow operator / automation specialist;
- niche AI-enabled product or service;
- hybrid human-AI service business.

## Opportunity record
Customer problem, target segment, service promise, required domain expertise, AI capability, human accountability, tools, startup cost, distribution, pricing hypothesis, legal constraints, competitive substitutes and validation test.

## Validation
Interview potential customers, test willingness to pay, deliver manually assisted pilot, measure outcomes, then automate repeatable parts. Do not assume AI capability creates demand.

## Career transitions
Map transferable skills, new skills, credentials, portfolio evidence, entry route and market demand. Distinguish emerging roles from speculative job titles.

## Example pattern
Electrician + AI may improve quoting, customer communication and documentation; a separate business opportunity could be a specialized digital service for small electrical contractors. The latter requires customer validation and is not guaranteed by tool availability.

## Acceptance criteria
Every opportunity includes a real customer problem and validation plan, not just a futuristic job title.
