# Experience & Seniority Specification

## Principle
Years alone do not define seniority.

Use:
task complexity, autonomy, scope, decision authority, ownership, mentoring, business impact, breadth, credentials.

## Levels
no experience / beginner / junior / intermediate / senior / lead / principal-expert / manager / director-executive / owner.

Not every occupation needs every level.

## Separate
occupation-specific experience / adjacent experience / transferable experience.

## Transferability
direct / adjacent / weak / none.

## Career switch
current expertise → transferable capital → new requirements → delta.

## Confidence
explicit / inferred / uncertain.

## Overqualification
Detect possible income/autonomy/entry-barrier trade-offs without judging the user.
