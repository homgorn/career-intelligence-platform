# Monetization & GTM Specification

## Models
Free basic profile/quiz.
One-time detailed career report.
Subscription for market updates, jobs, coach and application tracking.
B2B for universities, career centers, workforce programs, employers, outplacement.
Marketplace later.

## Integrity
Paid providers must not rank higher because they pay. Sponsored results must be explicit.

## Likely funnel
free quiz → useful profile → detailed report → action plan → job matching → optional coaching.

## Local wedge
Local salary, education, language, migration/remote paths and local employers can differentiate, but TAM and willingness-to-pay must be validated.

## B2B2C
Potentially lower CAC through institutions; validate procurement cycle.
