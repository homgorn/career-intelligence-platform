# Profession Module Template

## Identity
canonical name, aliases, category, parent occupation, localization names.

## Work
mission, tasks, work activities, context, environment, customer contact, autonomy, physical demands, travel, schedule.

## Entry
minimum skills, education, credential, license, experience, language.

## Skills
required level, necessary/defining/distinguishing, seniority relevance, evidence examples, transferability.

## Tools
software, hardware, equipment, platforms.

## Seniority
entry, junior, mid, senior, lead, manager.

## Specializations
branches and differentiators.

## Market
per geography: demand, salary, competition, openings, growth, remote share.

## Transitions
feeder occupations, adjacent occupations, exit occupations, transition difficulty.

## Learning
prerequisites, shortest path, standard path, advanced path.

## Assessment
5–20 occupation-specific diagnostic questions, practical scenarios, preference questions, evidence requests.

## Recommendation rules
hard exclusions, strong positives, weak positives, caution flags.

## Evidence
source/version/date for every factual field.
