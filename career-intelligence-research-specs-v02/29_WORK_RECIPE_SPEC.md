# AI Work Recipe

The core product unit: a reusable, evidence-backed recipe that connects a person's real task to a practical intervention and measured result.

## Schema

recipe_id; occupation; seniority; user_context; task; current_method; pain; desired_outcome; solution_type; tools; setup; skill_prerequisites; instructions; verification; risk; evidence; expected_value; observed_outcomes; maintenance.

## Solution types

NON_AI; HUMAN; AI_AUGMENT; AI_AUTOMATION; AGENT; HYBRID.

## Lifecycle

Draft → reviewed → verified → recommended → implemented → outcome-measured → versioned.

## Ranking

Expected outcome × evidence confidence × feasibility ÷ cost/risk/friction.
