# Privacy and data-subject controls

User-facing controls for personal and professional data.

## Controls

View, export, correct, delete, revoke consent where applicable, manage connected tools, inspect active sessions, manage communication preferences.

## Sensitive resume data

Minimize collection; redact where feasible; avoid using resume content for unrelated model training unless separately authorized and legally permitted.

## Enterprise

Tenant-level retention, region, SSO, SCIM where justified, admin roles, audit export, DPA workflow, subprocessor visibility.

## Privacy by design

Collect only fields that change a decision or improve a measurable outcome. Every profile field needs a product justification.
