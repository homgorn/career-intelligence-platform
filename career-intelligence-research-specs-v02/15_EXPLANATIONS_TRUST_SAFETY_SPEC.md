# Explanations, Trust & Safety Specification

## Inspectability
Every recommendation must expose:
profile evidence, matching factors, constraints, market evidence, missing data, uncertainty.

## Labels
stated / extracted / verified / inferred / estimated.

## Sensitive attributes
Do not use protected characteristics for exclusion without legal/ethical justification.

## Regulated professions
Medicine, law, aviation, electrical, transport, construction, finance require jurisdiction/credential caveats.

## No deterministic promises
Use «strong option based on current evidence», not «you will succeed».

## Market claims
source + date + geography + methodology.

## User control
edit weights, exclude occupations, add preferences, reject recommendations, request alternatives.

## Privacy
consent, deletion, export, retention, access control, encryption, no silent secondary use.
