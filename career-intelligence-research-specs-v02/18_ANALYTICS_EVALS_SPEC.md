# Analytics & Evaluation Specification

## Funnel
landing → quiz start → completion → CV → profile → recommendations → plan → job click → application → interview → offer → retention.

## Recommendation quality
expert agreement, ranking stability, coverage, calibration, constraint violation rate.

## Outcome metrics
application, interview, offer, hired, user-reported usefulness.

## Primary north-star candidate
Career decision quality / action completion, not CTR.

## Fairness
Measure performance across geography, language and education groups.

## Data quality
stale occupation data, duplicate skills, conflicting market data, missing salary, extraction errors, unsupported inference.

## Human evaluation
experts rate plausibility, factuality, explainability and actionability.
