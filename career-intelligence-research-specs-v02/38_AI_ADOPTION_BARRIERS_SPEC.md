# AI Adoption Barriers Specification

## Purpose
Diagnose why a person is not adopting AI even when a tool appears technically suitable.

## Barrier taxonomy
- awareness: does not know what is possible;
- comprehension: jargon or concepts are unclear;
- confidence: fear of making mistakes or looking incompetent;
- relevance: examples do not match the profession;
- trust: concerns about correctness or privacy;
- access: device, internet, account, payment or regional block;
- capability: digital literacy or language barrier;
- workflow: no clear place to insert AI;
- organization: no permission, manager support or process owner;
- economics: subscription or setup cost too high;
- maintenance: no time or support to keep it working;
- identity: concern about professional status or job displacement.

## Diagnostic method
Use behavior and task scenarios, not labels such as "resistant". Ask what happened during the last attempt. Distinguish lack of interest from lack of access or poor product fit.

## Intervention mapping
Each barrier maps to a specific intervention: demo, glossary, guided trial, local language content, offline path, privacy explainer, manager approval kit, low-cost package, human support or alternative tool.

## Metrics
First-task completion, time-to-first-value, abandonment stage, setup failure, repeat use, support requests and confidence after verified success.

## Ethics
Do not manipulate fear of job loss or create artificial urgency. Never frame non-adoption as personal failure.
