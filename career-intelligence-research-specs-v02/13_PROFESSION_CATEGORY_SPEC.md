# Profession Category / Subcategory Module Specification

## Why modules
Universal quiz cannot assess electrician, doctor, programmer, truck driver, teacher and designer equally well.

## Universal core
occupation metadata, tasks, skills, work context, entry requirements, seniority, market, adjacent roles.

## Skilled trades
tools, physical work, sites, safety, certification, apprenticeship, diagnostics, travel/weather.

## Healthcare
licensing, patient contact, shifts, clinical responsibility, credential recognition.

## IT
stack, portfolio, coding depth, system design, toolchain, remote viability.

## Finance
financial domain, regulation, Excel/ERP, certification, analytical work.

## Creative
portfolio, aesthetic preference, client feedback, tools, commercial constraints.

## Management
people scope, decision authority, budget, planning, stakeholder management.

## Sales
prospecting, negotiation, quota, segment, commission tolerance.

## Versioning
module version, evidence source, review date, expert owner, validation status.

## Data maturity
data-rich / data-moderate / data-poor. Do not claim equal precision across occupations.
