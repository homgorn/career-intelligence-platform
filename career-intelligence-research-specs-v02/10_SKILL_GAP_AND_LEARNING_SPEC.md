# Skill Gap & Learning Recommendation Specification

## Gap types
missing, weak, outdated, unverified, transferable, emerging, optional, differentiating.

## Priority
market demand × role importance × current gap × feasibility × user goal.

## Recommendation
skill, why needed, current/target level, 1–3 learning options, time, cost, evidence required, alternatives.

## Anti-course-spam
Return 1–3 highest leverage actions.

## Provider ranking
relevance, cost, duration, quality evidence, credential value, local availability, language, prerequisites.

## Evidence after learning
artifact task, knowledge check, portfolio, credential, practical assessment.

## ROI
Numeric claims require dated source and methodology.
