## Critical competitive lesson
Aptly already combines 13,000+ careers, skills, path plans, jobs, resume, applications and AI coach. Indeed Career Scout combines quiz, job recommendations, salary/company insights, resume builder and mock interviews.

Therefore «всё в одном» itself is not differentiation.

Better positioning:
**«что мне делать дальше, почему и каким путём»**.
