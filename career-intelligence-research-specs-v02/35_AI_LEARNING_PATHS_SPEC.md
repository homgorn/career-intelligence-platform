# AI Learning Paths Specification

## Goal
Teach only what the person needs to perform their work safely and effectively.

## Path structure
Skill outcome → micro-lesson → worked example → guided practice → independent task → verification → spaced refresher.

## Levels
- L0: digital basics and AI orientation;
- L1: safe everyday assistant use;
- L2: profession-specific copilot workflows;
- L3: multi-step workflows and integrations;
- L4: automation/agent supervision;
- L5: design, evaluation and governance (technical/managerial track).

## Learning modes
Short text, annotated screenshots, video, interactive simulator, voice walkthrough, live workshop, mentor session, downloadable checklist and peer cohort.

## Personalization
Use profession, seniority, preferred language, device, literacy, time budget, learning style preference and observed mistakes. Do not infer ability from age, geography or education alone.

## Curriculum design
- explain one concept at a time;
- show the result before the theory;
- use profession-realistic but safe examples;
- teach verification and data protection from lesson one;
- include "when not to use AI";
- avoid obsolete prompt tricks tied to one vendor.

## Assessment
Practical demonstration and rubric: task framing, context, source handling, verification, privacy, recovery. Course completion is not proof of competence.

## Content maintenance
Every lesson has owner, source, last checked date, product version and review cadence. Retire screenshots and instructions after UI changes.

## Acceptance criteria
Each learning path maps to an actual workflow and ends with a measurable ability, not vague familiarity with AI.
