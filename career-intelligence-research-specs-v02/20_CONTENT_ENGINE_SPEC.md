# Content operating system

Content is a productized research and distribution pipeline, not a publishing quota.

## Pipeline

Question → research → evidence → expert review → content → reusable structured entities → landing/workflow → distribution → performance → update.

## Formats

Guides; profession pages; task pages; tool comparisons; workflow recipes; case studies; calculators; benchmark reports; videos; newsletters; templates.

## Quality

Originality, evidence, expert review, update date, clear limitations, author/owner, user intent match.

## Repurposing

One verified research unit can feed web copy, product knowledge, email, social, video and sales collateral without changing the underlying factual claim.
