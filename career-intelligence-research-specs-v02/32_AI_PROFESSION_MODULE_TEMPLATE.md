# AI Profession Module Template

## 1. Profession identity
Canonical title, taxonomy IDs, country, language, specializations, seniority variants, regulated status.

## 2. Who performs this work?
Typical contexts: employee, freelancer, owner, manager, apprentice, contractor. Include company-size variants.

## 3. Task map
Task families, workflows, frequency ranges, tools, physical/digital/interpersonal components and evidence.

## 4. AI transformation map
For each task: assist / partial automation / agent candidate / human-led / not suitable. Explain why and cite evidence.

## 5. Starter workflows
At least three low-risk, high-frequency workflows with beginner and advanced paths.

## 6. Tools
Primary option, no-cost/low-cost alternative, professional option, integration and regional constraints. Add verification date.

## 7. Learning
Beginner micro-lessons, practical exercises, documentation, assessments and domain-specific safety training.

## 8. Implementation packages
Do-it-yourself, guided setup and done-for-you options with scope and prerequisites.

## 9. Skills and readiness
Professional skills, AI skills, adoption barriers, digital readiness and missing prerequisites.

## 10. Economics
Baseline, cost ranges, expected value ranges, break-even conditions and uncertainty.

## 11. Risks
Privacy, safety, legal, quality, bias, liability, overreliance and vendor lock-in.

## 12. New opportunities
New services, adjacent roles, AI-enabled business models, emerging skills and transition pathways.

## 13. Measurement
Baseline metrics, pilot protocol, quality rubric, feedback loop and retirement criteria.

## 14. Content metadata
Sources, evidence dates, author/reviewer, confidence, version, review date and known gaps.
