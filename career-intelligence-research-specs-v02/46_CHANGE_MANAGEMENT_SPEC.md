# Change Management & Adoption Specification

## Purpose
Help individuals and teams change the way work is done without treating tool installation as successful adoption.

## Change steps
1. map current work and stakeholders;
2. choose one low-risk pilot;
3. explain why it is changing and what remains human-owned;
4. involve users in design;
5. train through real tasks;
6. pilot with a baseline and feedback;
7. revise process and documentation;
8. roll out by role;
9. monitor workload, quality and unintended consequences;
10. review and retire ineffective workflows.

## Team readiness
Role clarity, process owner, training time, management support, worker consultation, data policy, escalation and feedback channels.

## Job impact communication
Describe task changes and support for reskilling. Do not promise no job impact or exaggerate replacement risk.

## Acceptance criteria
A team rollout includes a change owner, communication plan, training, worker feedback and post-launch review.
