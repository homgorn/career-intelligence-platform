# Legal and compliance gates

Legal architecture must be jurisdiction-aware. This is a product specification, not legal advice; counsel should validate final terms, notices and processing bases before launch.

## Data lifecycle

Collection notice → consent/other lawful basis where required → purpose limitation → processing → user access/export → correction → deletion → retention exception if legally required.

## Contract layers

Terms of Service; Privacy Notice; Cookie/Tracking Notice; DPA for business customers where applicable; subprocessor list; acceptable use; AI disclosure; enterprise security addendum.

## AI governance

Risk classification by use case; human oversight; logging; transparency; prohibited/restricted use cases; vendor due diligence; model change management. Map obligations by target market before launch.

## Employment context

Career and hiring features can become high-impact if used to make employment decisions. The product must distinguish career guidance for an individual from automated employment decision-making by an employer and route higher-risk use cases through legal review.

## Evidence

Maintain jurisdiction matrix with effective date, source, interpretation owner and implementation control. Never hard-code legal conclusions into prompts.
