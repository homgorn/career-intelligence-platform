# User / Career Profile Specification

## Identity/context
country, region/city, languages, work authorization, migration preference.

## Current state
occupation, employment status, experience, industry, seniority, employment type, income, schedule.

## Experience
occupation, employer/industry, dates, responsibilities, achievements, tools, team/scope, level, evidence.

## Education
degree, field, institution, year, vocational training, courses, apprenticeship.

## Skills
canonical skill, proficiency, years, last used, frequency, context, evidence, confidence.

## Credentials
certificate/license, issuer, jurisdiction, issue/expiry, verification, scope.

## Preferences
income, schedule, remote/hybrid/on-site, travel, relocation, shifts, overtime, physical work, outdoor work, customer-facing, team/solo.

## Constraints
hard / soft / temporary / unknown. «Предпочитаю» never becomes «не могу».

## Motivation
income, stability, autonomy, growth, status, creativity, social impact, flexibility, learning, low stress.

## Goals
stay, promotion, specialization, career switch, industry switch, country switch, freelance, entrepreneurship, part-time.

## Evidence hierarchy
verified credential/employer evidence > artifact > CV > structured interview > self-report > model inference.
