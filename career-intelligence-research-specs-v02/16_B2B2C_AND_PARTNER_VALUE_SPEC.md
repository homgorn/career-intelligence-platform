# B2B2C and partner ecosystem

Distribution through organizations and experts without sacrificing the user's control of personal career data.

## Channels

Universities; bootcamps; employers; recruitment agencies; career coaches; consultants; professional associations; government employment programs.

## Partner objects

Partner profile, audience, offer, attribution rules, lead ownership, payout, compliance status, certification, performance and revocation.

## Revenue

Referral fee; seat licensing; implementation revenue share; marketplace commission; white-label where strategically justified.

## Data boundary

Partner receives only the minimum data required for the contracted workflow. User consent and tenant policy govern data sharing.
