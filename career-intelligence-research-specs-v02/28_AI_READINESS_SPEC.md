# AI Readiness Specification

## Purpose
Determine whether a person, workflow or organization can adopt an AI solution now, and what must be fixed first.

## Readiness dimensions
- user AI literacy and confidence;
- task digitization;
- data quality and availability;
- existing software and integrations;
- process clarity and standardization;
- device, connectivity and accessibility;
- budget and time;
- privacy, legal and security constraints;
- management support and process ownership;
- tolerance for errors and ability to verify;
- support capacity and continuity.

## Readiness levels
0 — no baseline / no digital workflow;
1 — exploratory use;
2 — repeatable individual copilot use;
3 — documented team workflow;
4 — integrated automation with controls;
5 — monitored, measured and continuously improved system.

Levels are descriptive, not a maturity badge. A high-risk task can remain human-led at any level.

## Diagnostic
Ask about actual work and demonstrate a small task. Do not quiz users on acronyms. Identify blockers and propose a low-cost remediation path.

## Output
Readiness profile, blockers, safe pilot candidates, prerequisites, recommended package, excluded use cases and review date.

## Acceptance criteria
The system must distinguish user readiness from technical feasibility and business value. A task can be technically feasible but not ready because data, permissions or process ownership are missing.
