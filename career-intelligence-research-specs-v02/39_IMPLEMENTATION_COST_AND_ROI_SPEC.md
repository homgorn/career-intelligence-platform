# Implementation Cost & ROI Specification

## Cost components
- discovery and process mapping;
- subscriptions and usage-based model/API costs;
- connector/integration fees;
- setup and configuration;
- data cleaning and migration;
- training and change management;
- human review and exception handling;
- maintenance, monitoring and support;
- security/compliance work;
- switching and exit costs.

## Cost model
Show one-time, monthly fixed, variable per task and internal labor costs separately. Include taxes/currency and pricing check date where known.

## ROI model
`Net monthly benefit = measurable monthly value − total monthly cost`.
`Payback period = one-time implementation cost / positive monthly net benefit`.

Use scenarios and disclose assumptions. If benefit is non-financial (quality, safety, accessibility), report it separately rather than forcing a monetary value.

## Sensitivity analysis
Vary task volume, adoption rate, review time, error/rework rate, subscription cost and implementation effort.

## No-benefit case
Show when not to buy: low frequency, cheap manual task, high verification burden, poor data, high risk, no owner or no repeatability.

## Acceptance criteria
No ROI claim without baseline, cost inclusion, assumptions, period and uncertainty range.
