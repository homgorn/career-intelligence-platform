# SEO and programmatic search architecture

Search strategy centered on useful entity/workflow pages rather than mass-produced pages.

## Page graph

Occupation × task × problem × AI/non-AI option × tool × workflow × learning × implementation × case.

## Page quality gate

A programmatic page may ship only if it has unique structured data, useful explanation, source/provenance, decision guidance, and meaningful differentiation from sibling pages.

## Technical SEO

Canonical URLs; indexability; sitemap segmentation; robots; structured data; internal linking; hreflang; performance; accessibility; pagination strategy; monitoring.

## AI search

Follow people-first and technical search fundamentals. Do not build the strategy around speculative AEO/GEO hacks or fabricated authority.
