# Sales, CRM and revenue operations

Revenue system for self-serve B2C and sales-assisted B2B.

## Lead states

Anonymous → known → qualified → diagnostic complete → opportunity → proposal → closed won/lost → activated → outcome.

## Scoring

Score fit, intent, urgency, value potential, implementation readiness and risk. Avoid using sensitive personal attributes as hidden sales signals.

## CRM data

Account, contact, role, use case, opportunity, stage, next action, product usage, outcome, partner attribution, consent.

## Sales enablement

ROI calculator; transformation assessment; security pack; implementation plan; case study; proposal generator; objection handling.
